Field guide

How to play

1 · Move

Your spark follows your cursor or finger, trailing a ribbon of light.

2 · Loop

Cross your own ribbon to close a loop. Every shadow inside is bound.

3 · Combo

More shadows per loop = bigger bonus. Bind quickly to raise your ×multiplier.

4 · Nova

Stardust charges your Nova. Unleash it to bind everything around you.
